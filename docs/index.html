INCOMPLETE TIMING STUDY

Four attempted automated runs produced no valid comparison.

The raw benchmark retains all four failed attempts. Each failed before producing a valid deliverable, and no comparable manual completions have been observed. The current data therefore cannot estimate workflow time reduction. The predeclared study requires at least five quality-validated automated runs across three persona selections and three comparable manual completions.

Offline fixtures validate instrumentation only and are excluded from timing estimates.

Boundaries that matter

  • Persona affinities are useful prototype scores, not calibrated probabilities of persona truth.
  • The bundled 50-row dataset demonstrates workflow behavior; it does not establish broad market validity.
  • The labeled deduplication fixture is deliberately small and does not imply production-wide accuracy.
  • Generated market pains are hypotheses for review, not verified facts about every account in a persona.
